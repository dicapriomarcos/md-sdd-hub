## 1. Context

Which experience or interface problem needs solving, for whom, and in which screens or flows it appears.

## 2. Decision

What is decided, concretely and actionably: screens, flows, components, states (empty, loading, error), styles, copy, accessibility, mobile behavior.

## 3. Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why it is discarded |
|---|---|
| Option A | Reason |

## 4. Consequences

- What improves for the user.
- What gets worse or what it costs.
- What must be respected from now on in other screens.

## 5. Interface rules

- Concrete rule the AI must apply when building screens (e.g. "destructive buttons always ask for confirmation").

## 6. References

Mockups, screenshots, links to Figma or to the design system documentation.
